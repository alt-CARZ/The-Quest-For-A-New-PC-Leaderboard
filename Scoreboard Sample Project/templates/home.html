About The Game

The Quest For A PC is a fast-paced indie game that has taken the internet by storm, earning praise from top YouTubers like Markiplier and reviewers from The New York Times.

Your ultimate goal? To buy a powerful new PC to play Geometry Jump -- an incredibly graphics-heavy game that requires split-second precision. Navigate an open-world internet packed with intricate puzzles and challenges. Track your stats, unlock unique achievements, and make choices carefully -- because every decision counts.



How to play

  1. Interact and Explore


    Use your mouse to browse, interact, and download files on a completely procedurally generated internet, in this point and click game



  2. Solve and Adapt


    Overcome interactive puzzles and learn complex digital survival techniques as you progress. The introductory tutorial chapter teaches you all the baseline skills required to get that PC you need so desperately.



  3. Upgrade and Hunt


    Unlock harder files, bypass superior security systems, and dig up the cheapest hardware deals online to build your dream machine.




Educational purpose

The Quest For A PC provides a safe, sandbox simulation to help you recognize and defeat real-world digital threats.



What you can learn!


  • Spotting threats: Learn to find and overcome malware, scams, and phishers.

  • Master defence: Learn to white hat hack and implement it within controlled, safe environments.

  • Code your own site: Unlock online interactivity, create your own website, and host it on our sandbox internet for free!

  • Challenge friends: Create websites that challenge friends to test their white hat hacking, or danger evasion abilities!
