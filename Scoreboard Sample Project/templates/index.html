Username Help


The username is the name that will be displayed on the scoreboard. It can be any name you like, but it must be unique. If you repeat a username, your best entry will override your past entries.



Time Help


The time is the time it took you to complete the game. It must be a number between 0 and 9999, and at most 2 decimal places. If you enter a time that is not a number, or is outside of this range, your entry will be rejected.



Multiplier Help


The multiplier is your time multiplier that is given to you by the game at the end of the run. If it does not, you can calculate it with:


Multiplier = 1 + (fails * 0.1) - (grade * 0.1)

Fails is the fail count of the entire run, displayed at the end of the game.
The grade is your numeric rating of performance. You gain +1 on your grade for every achievement and extra content you have successfully obtained in the run. If you forget the grade you got, just count the number of achievements and extra content you obtained.
